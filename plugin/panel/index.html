One-Click Raw Cut

Detects silences & breathing pauses locally (ffmpeg/VAD — no API costs) and builds a new timeline "<name> [AI Raw Cut]". Your original timeline is never modified — to undo, simply delete the generated timeline.


      

    

  


  
  

    

      
SFX / VFX Library

      
Connect folders of sound effects / video assets. The
        plugin indexes them locally (filename + duration + heuristic tags,
        cached — never re-sent to any API). Then get a recommended SFX
        list for your timeline, or auto-insert them on a new
        audio track.


      

      
      

        
        
      

    


    

      
Recommend SFX for this timeline

      
Reads the cut points on your current timeline and maps
        them to matching SFX (hook → riser/impact, shot change → transition).

      
      

        
        
      

      
Auto-insert is additive — it adds
        a dedicated SFX audio track and never deletes anything. Undo via
        Resolve Undo or by deleting that track. (Untested on real Resolve.)
